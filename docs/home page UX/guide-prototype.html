Is it cheaper to bundle broadband and TV?

Usually, if you watch what you pay for. Ofcom found bundlers typically pay £26 to £48 a month less than buying services separately.

£26–£48a month saved on average by bundling
23%real-terms fall in pay-TV cost within bundles, to about £12/mo

A large bundle with sport and cinema packs you rarely watch isn't a saving. It's a bigger bill with extra channels attached. Use Ofcom's figures as a sense check on a live quote, not a promise.

Source: Ofcom, "New research reveals how to cut phone and internet bills", February 2026.

How do I compare the true cost of a bundle?

Add up every monthly payment for the whole contract, plus fees, minus guaranteed credits. Then divide by the months.

Total cost = all monthly payments + setup and delivery fees + required add-ons − guaranteed credits

  • Check the broadband: compare the guaranteed or estimated speed at your address, not the advertised maximum.
  • List must-have content: name the exact sports, channels and apps you watch.
  • Read the price-rise wording: Virgin Media's Max Volt rises to £81.49 from March 2027 and £87.99 from April 2028. EE adds £6 a month each 31 March.

What broadband speed do I need for streaming TV?

Sky Stream needs at least 25Mbps, or 30Mbps for UHD. Busy homes need more.

Add every simultaneous stream, video call, game download and connected device. A stable 50–100Mbps service suits many small households; busy multi-user homes may benefit from faster full fibre. Reliability and Wi-Fi coverage can matter more than the top advertised speed.

Does One Touch Switch cancel my old TV service?

Not always. One Touch Switch moves your broadband, but Ofcom warns you may still need to cancel a linked TV service yourself.

Check exit charges, equipment returns and each service's end date before confirming. New orders usually have a 14-day cooling-off period.

Frequently asked questions

Who has the best broadband and TV deals in the UK?

It depends on what you watch. Sky suits TV-first homes, Virgin Media suits fast broadband with a big channel line-up where it's available, and EE TV suits TNT Sports fans and people who want to change packs.

Which broadband and TV provider is best for sport?

EE's Big Sport pack combines Sky Sports (via NOW) and TNT Sports. Virgin Media's Max Volt includes Sky Sports HD. Compare the total contract cost, as sports packs are the most expensive add-on.

Can I get Sky TV with broadband from another provider?

Yes. Sky channels are available through NOW and through EE TV packs, so you don't have to take Sky broadband to watch them.

What should I compare besides the monthly price?

Total contract cost, scheduled price rises, setup fees, the speed available at your address, and whether TV and broadband end on the same date.

What is Virgin Media Max Volt?

A bundle of gigabit-boosted broadband, an O2 5G SIM with unlimited data, Virgin TV 360 with 230+ channels including Sky Sports HD and Sky Cinema HD, and free UK calls. It's from £74.99 a month, with rises already scheduled for 2027 and 2028.
