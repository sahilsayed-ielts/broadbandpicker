```
If the package does not say full fibre or FTTP, copper may still be in the mix.

A lot of UK advertising still says fibre when the last stretch is copper from a cabinet (FTTC). Full fibre, or FTTP, is glass all the way to the premises.

It is usually stronger on upload and less likely to fall apart at 7pm. If a package does not say full fibre or FTTP, assume copper may still be in the mix.
```
