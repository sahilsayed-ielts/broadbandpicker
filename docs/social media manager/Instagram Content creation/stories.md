# Instagram Stories

Professional weekday habit, not a sticker playground. 1080x1920, 5 seconds a frame, navy or paper. Type sits above the sticker tray.

## Recurring series

| Day | Series | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Bill check | Poll: in contract or out of contract? Then one fact about intro versus out-of-contract price. |
| Tuesday | Tech | This or that: full fibre or cheaper cabinet fibre. One labelled diagram. |
| Wednesday | Speed | Slider: what do you pay. Then the household Mbps ranges. |
| Thursday | Switch | Question sticker: still think you have to phone them? One Touch Switch frame. |
| Friday | Useful | One guide cover. Link sticker to that guide. No more than one link per set. |
| Saturday | Buffering watch | If a big fixture is on, a calm 'evening speed' reminder. Not a meme dump. |
| Sunday | Admin | If you do one useful thing this week: check the postcode. Link sticker. |

## Highlight frames to film on day 0

- **About** — 1) Independent UK broadband comparison. 2) Postcode first. 3) Rankings not sold. 4) Link to /about.
- **How it works** — 1) Enter postcode. 2) Compare price, speed, contract. 3) Switch. 4) How we make money, one frame.
- **Switch** — 1) You pick the deal. 2) New provider handles the move. 3) Check exit fees. 4) Guide link.
- **Full fibre** — 1) Fibre on the advert. 2) Copper from the cabinet. 3) Glass to the house. 4) Check the postcode.
- **Tariffs** — 1) They exist. 2) £12.50 to £24 band. 3) Most eligible households have not heard. 4) Guide link.
- **Deals** — 1) Intro versus out of contract. 2) Speed is typical, not 'up to' as a promise. 3) Setup fees. 4) /deals.
- **Guides** — 1) Speeds. 2) Switching. 3) Social tariffs. 4) Cheapest broadband UK. Link stickers.

## Hygiene

- No more than one link sticker per set.
- Never screenshot a customer's bill with a visible address.
- Do not save joke replies into Highlights until the seven professional covers exist.
