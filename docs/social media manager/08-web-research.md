## Competitor gap

Uswitch, MoneySuperMarket, Compare the Market, broadbandchoices and choose.co.uk talk like comparison engines. MoneySavingExpert talks like a consumer champion. Which? talks like a lab. None of them run a consistently funny, design-led UK broadband brand on TikTok. Instagram is the professional shopfront: providers judge the grid in three seconds. Which? has already called out ISPs for barely posting about social tariffs. X and TikTok take the sarcasm. Instagram takes the publisher look.

## Instagram professional setup (scraped)

Instagram is the professional shopfront for BroadbandPicker. X and TikTok can be rude. The IG grid, bio and highlights have to look like Which? crossed with Octopus: calm, useful, independent, navy/sky, no SALE energy.

Live IG profile fetches often hit a login wall. Curated bios used when the scrape is thin: Monzo ("Hi, we're Monzo. We're a bank that lives on your phone."), Octopus Energy (better energy for you, wallet, planet). Full rules and paste-ready bio: `Instagram Content creation/account-setup.md`.
