### Overview (1405 / 2000)

```
Independent UK broadband comparison. Enter a postcode, see fibre and full fibre deals that can actually serve that address, then pick by price, speed and contract.

BroadbandPicker is a free comparison site for UK households. We compare packages from 24 providers, from BT, Sky, Virgin Media, EE, TalkTalk, Vodafone and Plusnet through to regional full fibre names such as Hyperoptic, Community Fibre and Toob. Tables cover monthly price, typical speed, contract length and setup fees. Rankings are not sold.

Availability is by address, never by a national advert. Two houses on the same road can see different networks, so a "from £19" headline is not a deal until you check the postcode.

We also publish provider reviews and plain English guides on switching (including One Touch Switch), full fibre versus cabinet fibre, social tariffs, and what speed a household actually needs. Scoring draws on live provider pricing, Ofcom Connected Nations data and Ofcom complaints figures.

The site is free. If you click Get Deal and sign up, we may earn an affiliate commission. That does not buy a higher rank, and we still list providers we do not earn from. Affiliate relationships are disclosed. We work through the Awin network.

If you work at an ISP, altnet or affiliate network and want a listing conversation, write to partnerships@broadbandpicker.co.uk.

Compare deals: https://broadbandpicker.co.uk
```

First 150-200 characters are the search snippet. Full paste is in LinkedIn/account-setup.md
