### X-001 — Loyalty is a hostage situation

- **Pillar:** sarcasm
- **Hook:** Been with them 8 years.
- **Best on:** x, instagram, tiktok · tweet, reel, story, tiktok, carousel
- **Why it should travel:** Loyalty-tax rage is the most shareable broadband emotion in Britain. People tag the bill-payer.
- **Engagement mechanic:** Quote this with what you actually pay. No judgement. Well, a bit of judgement.
- **CTA / URL:** Check you're not the business model: https://broadbandpicker.co.uk/deals
- **Hashtags (use sparingly):** #UKBroadband
- **Compliance:** Do not invent a specific provider price. Talk about the industry pattern. Deals vary by postcode.

**Copy**

Been with the same broadband lot for 8 years.
New customers: £19.
Me: £41.

That's not loyalty. That's a hostage situation with slightly better Wi-Fi.

If you're out of contract you're not a valued customer. You're the business model.

**Alt / shorter**

Out of contract on broadband is just a standing order you forgot to argue with.

**Design brief**

**Format:** Split bill meme / end card
**Dimensions:** 1080x1350 feed, 1080x1920 Reel/TikTok, 1600x900 for X
**Duration:** 0:07–0:12 video or static
**Visual concept:** Two fake bills side by side. Left labelled NEW CUSTOMER in sky blue. Right labelled LOYAL CUSTOMER in angry red-navy. Same tiny print energy as a real ISP email.
**On-screen text:**
  - New customer: £19
  - You: £41
  - That's not loyalty.
  - That's a hostage situation.
**Palette:** Navy #0F172A background, sky #0EA5E9 highlights, white type, green #22C55E only for a genuine 'good news' sting. High contrast, no grey-on-grey.
**Type:** Anton or Impact for the joke; Inter Bold for the end card.
**Logo:** Native joke first, brand last. Wifi-mark + wordmark on a 0.4s end card or bottom-left safe zone. Never put the logo over the punchline.
**Audio:** Dry VO, northern or estuary, unimpressed. Optional: hold-music sting at the end.
**Shot list:**
  - 0-2s bills slam on
  - 2-7s punchline types on
  - 7-12s BroadbandPicker end card + URL
**Do not:**
  - Don't use a real customer's bill
  - Don't name a fake official tariff
