## Positioning

BroadbandPicker should post as an independent UK broadband comparison brand that helps people:
- compare deals by postcode
- understand speeds, contracts, and switching
- save money on broadband

## Recommended Cadence

- 4 posts per week to start
- Best posting windows: `8:30 AM`, `12:30 PM`, `6:00 PM` UK time
- Mix:
  - 2 educational posts
  - 1 deal/comparison post
  - 1 trust/editorial post
