## Reasoning
Category fit is strong (7/10) — a pure residential full-fibre ISP with sharp fixed pricing and segment angles that map onto content you already publish — but the footprint is small, so eligible traffic and revenue are capped regardless of terms. The commercial evidence is currently too thin to call: neither the invitation nor the T&Cs state any commission rate, so it cannot be benchmarked against the £50–£120 CPA norm for comparison sites (and as an altnet they will likely land at the low end). The 30-day cookie is standard; the missing validation and payment window is a real gap for a long-install product. The most serious issue is deduplication explicitly against price comparison sites, direct partnerships and their in-house programme — as a postcode-comparison site, that is precisely the mechanism by which your confirmed sales get clawed back to Connect Fibre's own PPC or listings. Joining a CPA programme has no downside, but building content before these points are resolved would be premature.

## Exact next step
Reply to Remon/the account manager before agreeing to anything, asking for in writing: (a) the commission amount per broadband sale and whether it varies by tier; (b) the validation lock period and payment timeframe; (c) explicit confirmation that comparison-site conversions are not routinely deduplicated against their in-house, direct or PCP activity, plus the notice commitment for any dedup-policy change; and (d) what the open-ended "Other" decline reason covers. Cite Awin's rule that Awin commission may not be set below what other channels or their in-house programme earn.

## Walk-away condition
If the CPA comes back materially below the ~£50 floor *and* they will not protect comparison-site attribution from dedup against their own channels, walk away — the narrow Midlands/East footprint means the effective revenue would not repay the content investment.
