Natural homes: "Best broadband for customer service," "Broadband with no mid-contract price rises," Which?-recommended roundups, full-fibre 900Mb–2Gb comparisons, gaming broadband, and business/static-IP guides. Include in switching and One Touch Switch explainers as the worked example. Pitch to Zen as a content-led editorial partner targeting quality-conscious switchers, not a voucher/cashback placement.

- Availability is Openreach/CityFibre-footprint dependent, so many postcode checks will dead-end.
