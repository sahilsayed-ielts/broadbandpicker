1. What's the commission rate, and is it a flat rate or tiered?
2. How long is the cookie window, and is it last-click?
3. Do UK and other non-US orders qualify? Do you ship to the UK, and in GBP?
4. What's the validation period, typical approval rate and payment schedule?
5. Are any promotional methods restricted, such as PPC brand bidding, voucher sites, email or social?
6. Is there any exclusivity, or a minimum traffic or sales requirement?
7. What's the policy on using LEGO® trademarks in affiliate content?
